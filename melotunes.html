How note recognition works
1. Find the five staff lines and their spacing.
2. Ignore the clef/key-signature area at the left of the staff.
3. Test each possible line/space position for a tilted oval notehead.
4. Ignore staff-line pixels when measuring the oval, so a line crossing a note does not split it.
5. Convert its center to a pitch, then estimate duration from the head, stem, and flag.

4. Detect and play
