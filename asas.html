THE MOVIE CATALOGUE

4 Fav Movies

My reccomended movies

Link Click (Chinese Series)

Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang enter photographs to relive moments from the past. Their ability comes with strict rules, and every decision risks changing what was meant to happen.

MY LITTLE REVIEW · NO. 01

Menurut aku, Link Click (Shiguang Dailiren) menarik banget karena konsep time travelnya! Ada aturan ketat kalau melompat ke masa lalu lewat foto tidak boleh mengubah takdir besar. Series ini sukses memicu ketegangan luar biasa saat rencana Lu Guang ternyata mulai melenceng. Setiap season selalu diberi plot twist yang bikin mikir keras dan momen yang emosional banget.

♡

Alice in Borderland (Japanese Series)

After finding themselves in an abandoned version of Tokyo, a group of friends must take on a series of dangerous games to earn the right to keep living in this strange world.

MY LITTLE REVIEW · NO. 02

Aku ***** Alice in Borderland karena genre thriller yang digabungkan dengan death game lewat teka-teki psikologis dan bikin otak ikut muter keras, jadi bukan cuma soal adu fisik aja! Suasana kota Tokyo yang kosong melompong plus permainan kejam di tiap episode selalu berhasil bikin deg-degan parah dan nagih.

♡

Re/member (Japanese Series)

Six high-school students become trapped in a repeating night and must work together to uncover the mystery behind it. Their growing trust becomes just as important as solving the challenge.

MY LITTLE REVIEW · NO. 03

Buat aku, Re/Member (Karada Sagashi) juga seru banget karena bukan cuma jualan teror kutukan yang mematikan. Tapi juga tentang ikatan persahabatan yang hangat di antara 6 siswa SMA yang awalnya saling asing dan kesepian. Mereka perlahan saling percaya dan kompak berjuang bareng demi memutus lingkaran waktu yang mengerikan. Bikin film ini terasa sangat emosional di tengah ketegangan permainannya.

♡

You Drive Me Crazy (Russian Movie)

A romantic story built around two parallel realities, where an unusual mirror connects the lives of people who would otherwise never meet.

MY LITTLE REVIEW · NO. 04

Genrenya sedikit beda, tapi You Drive Me Crazy (Svodish s uma) ini bikin konsep parallel universe terasa manis banget lewat cermin apartemen yang nyambungin 2 realitas berbeda. Melihat dua di beda dimensi ini pelan-pelan jatuh cinta walau tidak bisa saling sentuh bikin ceritanya terasa hangat dan menarik.
